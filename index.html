The FDE Model

How a Forward Deployed
Engineer works

01

Embed

We spend time inside your business before writing a single line of code or setting up a single tool. We learn how you operate, where you lose time, and where technology would create the most leverage.

02

Map

We document your current workflows and identify the exact gaps. You get a clear picture of what to build first, what to automate, and what to skip, with a transparent scope and cost before anything starts.

03

Deploy

We build and deploy directly for your environment. Not a generic template. Not a packaged product. Systems configured for your tools, your team, and the way your business actually works.

04

Iterate

An FDE does not disappear after go-live. We stay embedded, monitor what is working, and keep adapting the technology as your business grows and your needs change.
